Rooted in Tradition

Where the
Savannah
Meets Craft

Sero Leather Africa began with a simple vision — to elevate the extraordinary leatherworking tradition of Kenya onto the global stage. We started in a small Nairobi workshop with three artisans and a conviction that African craft deserves to be celebrated, not overlooked.

Every bag we make begins with a story rooted in the land — the deep reds of Kenyan soil, the golden savannah light at dusk, the enduring spirit of the people who shaped this place. We pour all of that into every stitch.

What Drives Us

Our Values

Uncompromising Quality

Every stitch is inspected. Every hide evaluated. We build leather goods designed to outlast trends and outlive expectations.

Empowering Communities

By employing local talent and paying fair wages, we invest directly back into the communities that inspire our designs every day.

Sustainable Practices

We use vegetable tanning and aim for zero waste production by upcycling every leather scrap into smaller accessories.
